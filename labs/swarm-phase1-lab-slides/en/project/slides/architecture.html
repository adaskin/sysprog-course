BIL 322 · Swarm Phase 1 lab

The center’s main thread runs the clock: it sends each section’s theme and each beat, and waits until every leader has said READY for a section and ACK for each beat. One reader thread per leader parses that leader’s pipe. The choreographer is a thread pool behind a bounded request queue, and every request takes memory from your Lab 6 pool. Each leader is a child process started with fork, dup2 and exec, like your Lab 4 shell. Inside it: a main thread reading the pipe, a thinker thread that asks for plans, and one thread per follower. The occupancy grid is per leader, because the followers are its threads and can claim cells directly. The center does not trust the leaders: it audits the POS reports for collisions and speed. Read center.c once from top to bottom before you write your part; its locks and their order are commented.
