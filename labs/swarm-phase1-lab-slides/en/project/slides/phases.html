BIL 322 · Swarm Phase 1 lab

The protocol stays the same in all phases; what changes is how the pieces talk. This week leaders are child processes created with fork and exec (your Lab 4 pattern) and connected by pipes (Lab 5). Performers are threads. Phase 2 moves the stage into shared memory and the viewer into its own process. In Phase 3 everything uses sockets and performers become separate processes, so when a captain dies its troupe is really orphaned. The question behind the whole project: when a captain dies, who notices, and who takes over its troupe? Your code already sends lines over file descriptors, so the later change of transport is small.
