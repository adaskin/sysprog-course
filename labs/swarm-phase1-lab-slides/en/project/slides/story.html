The project

A swarm of drones that dances to music

Hundreds of LED drones perform a choreography, then form a picture in the sky. Four roles make the show.

Center

Artistic Director

Reads the score and the target image, sets each section’s theme, runs the beat clock, hosts the slow planner.

Leaders

Dance Captains

One process per zone. Each asks for a plan, checks it, and directs its troupe.

Followers

Performers

Threads inside a leader in Phase 1. Each flies to a cell, lights a color, reports.

Viewer

Broadcast Booth

A separate GUI from Phase 2. It may drop frames, never slow the swarm.

BIL 322 · Swarm Phase 1 lab

The story: the engineering faculty takes a stadium tradition into the air. The center knows the score (the sections and moods of the music) and the target image, and decides only the coarse theme of each section. The detailed movements come from the choreographer, a slow thinker. In a real show it would be an LLM; here it is a stub that sleeps, so you feel what it is like to wait for a model that is slow, busy or wrong. Leaders are dance captains, one process per zone. Followers are cheap performers; in Phase 1 they are threads inside their captain. The viewer becomes a separate GUI in Phase 2. Today it is just the stage drawn as text.
