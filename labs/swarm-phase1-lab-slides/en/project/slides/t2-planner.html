The choreographer and the captain

Center

handle_plan_req, serve_request

  • try_push; queue full: BUSY 50. A reader thread never blocks
  • Semaphore: at most --max-inflight planner calls
  • KV blocks from the Lab 6 pool. The pool is not thread-safe: wrap it in pool_mu
  • Pool empty: BUSY. That is backpressure too
  • Every exit path gives back the blocks and posts the semaphore

Captain

thinker_main

  • Per THEME: send PLAN_REQ, wait for PLAN or BUSY
  • BUSY: sleep retry_ms + a random extra. A fixed delay makes every leader retry together: livelock
  • Run validate_plan. Invalid: new seed, at most 6 tries (--flaky 50 tests this)
  • Always send READY, even after giving up: the troupe holds and the show goes on

BIL 322 · Swarm Phase 1 lab

In the center, a reader thread receives PLAN_REQ and must never block: it does a try_push on the request queue, and if the queue is full it answers BUSY immediately. A planner thread pops the request, takes a semaphore slot (this limits how many planner calls run at once) and takes its KV blocks from your Lab 6 pool. Your pool is deliberately not thread-safe, so every alloc and free goes under pool_mu. If the pool is empty the answer is BUSY as well: two kinds of backpressure. Then plan_section runs (it sleeps to simulate a slow model), the PLAN is sent, and the blocks and the semaphore are released on every path. In the leader, the thinker asks for a plan per theme. After BUSY it waits retry_ms plus a random extra: if all leaders retried at the same moment they would collide again and again, which is livelock. A real model could be wrong, so the captain never trusts a plan: the validator checks speed, collisions, zone and density, and an invalid plan is requested again with a new seed. The flaky option makes half of the plans wrong on purpose. READY is sent even if the captain gave up; otherwise the center would wait for that leader forever.
