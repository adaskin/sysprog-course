BIL 322 · Swarm Phase 1 lab

The occupancy grid: one mutex for the whole grid versus one mutex per row. Implement claim, release and owner for both modes. A reader must lock too: without the lock it can see a half-updated state, which is a data race. make bench runs random claim and release from 1, 2, 4 and 8 threads in both modes. Put the table in your report and explain your own numbers: they depend on your machine and cores (contention on one mutex, what scales, and what else could be the bottleneck). The show itself at tempo 0 is dominated by the planner’s thinking time, so the grid is not the bottleneck there. Second part: follower_main with the two barriers from the beat slide.
